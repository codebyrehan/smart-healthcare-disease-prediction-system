09 · RESPONSIBLE AI

Responsible AI & Limitations

Transparent interpretation of this educational healthcare ML system.

  • The PIMA dataset is historical and limited to the population represented by its source; generalization to other populations is not guaranteed.
  • A model probability is not a diagnosis, prognosis, treatment recommendation, or causal estimate.
  • Feature importance describes model behavior and must not be interpreted as medical causation.
  • Results depend on data quality, preprocessing, model version and evaluation design.
