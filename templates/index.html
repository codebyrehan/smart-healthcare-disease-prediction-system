Responsible AI & Medical Governance

Ethical constraints, model limitations, and clinical usage scope.

  • Decision Support Only: This machine learning system is engineered for academic research, education, and decision support. It does not provide medical diagnosis or therapeutic prescriptions.
  • Population Specificity: Trained on the PIMA Indians Diabetes Dataset. Generalization to demographically distinct populations requires external validation.
  • Explainability Dynamics: Feature importance rankings reflect model mathematical weights and do not establish direct clinical causation.
  • Always Consult Certified Clinicians: Formal healthcare decisions must be guided by certified healthcare professionals and clinical laboratory tests.
